# How Painters Caught Light

Ten paintings from 1434 to 1889, each a new answer to one question: how do you put light on a flat surface? Told as a conversation between a guide and a curious visitor.

1. [How did oil paint make light glow? Van Eyck's Arnolfini Portrait](https://www.drawcast.app/#gh=hmelberg/drawcast-library/how-painters-caught-light/01-how-did-oil-paint-make-light-glow-van.cast)
   - Where does the light come from in this room?
   - What could oil glazes do that earlier paint couldn't?
2. [Can one beam tell a story? Caravaggio's Calling of Saint Matthew](https://www.drawcast.app/#gh=hmelberg/drawcast-library/how-painters-caught-light/02-can-one-beam-tell-a-story-caravaggio-s.cast)
   - Where does the beam enter, and what does it hit?
   - What does the darkness do?
3. [How does light pick out a hero? Rembrandt's The Night Watch](https://www.drawcast.app/#gh=hmelberg/drawcast-library/how-painters-caught-light/03-how-does-light-pick-out-a-hero-rembrandt.cast)
   - Who is lit, and who is left in shadow?
   - Who is the glowing girl?
4. [What can one candle do? La Tour's Magdalen with the Smoking Flame](https://www.drawcast.app/#gh=hmelberg/drawcast-library/how-painters-caught-light/04-what-can-one-candle-do-la-tour-s.cast)
   - What is lit, and what is hidden?
   - Why a candle and a skull?
5. [Why does window light feel so calm? Vermeer's The Milkmaid](https://www.drawcast.app/#gh=hmelberg/drawcast-library/how-painters-caught-light/05-why-does-window-light-feel-so-calm.cast)
   - Where does the light fall, and where doesn't it?
   - How do the little dots of light on the bread work?
6. [Can a sunset say goodbye? Turner's The Fighting Temeraire](https://www.drawcast.app/#gh=hmelberg/drawcast-library/how-painters-caught-light/06-can-a-sunset-say-goodbye-turner-s-the.cast)
   - What is the old ship, and what is towing it?
   - What is the sunset doing on the right?
7. [What if you paint the light, not the thing? Monet's Impression, Sunrise](https://www.drawcast.app/#gh=hmelberg/drawcast-library/how-painters-caught-light/07-what-if-you-paint-the-light-not-the.cast)
   - How little is actually drawn?
   - How did this painting give a movement its name?
8. [Why paint the same church thirty times? Monet's Rouen Cathedral](https://www.drawcast.app/#gh=hmelberg/drawcast-library/how-painters-caught-light/08-why-paint-the-same-church-thirty-times.cast)
   - What changes between two of the paintings?
   - What was Monet really painting?
9. [Can dots make light? Seurat's A Sunday on La Grande Jatte](https://www.drawcast.app/#gh=hmelberg/drawcast-library/how-painters-caught-light/09-can-dots-make-light-seurat-s-a-sunday-on.cast)
   - What happens to the dots when you step back?
   - Why did Seurat think this would make colours brighter?
10. [Can light be a feeling? Van Gogh's The Starry Night](https://www.drawcast.app/#gh=hmelberg/drawcast-library/how-painters-caught-light/10-can-light-be-a-feeling-van-gogh-s-the.cast)
   - What is real in this sky, and what isn't?
   - Where does the light come from now?
