I speak Russian: я говорю, ты говоришь

Ask a Russian if they speak English, answer "yes, a little", and see how one verb changes its ending for я, ты, он, она and вы.

How do you ask someone in Russian whether they speak English, and what do you say when they ask you back?

We're back in the café: I'm the waitress, Tom is my customer, so we say вы.

Говори́те means speak, so вы говори́те по-англи́йски asks: do you speak English?

There's no word for do: a question is the plain sentence, with the voice going up.

And the answer was да, немно́го: yes, a little. Да is yes, the partner of нет from lesson one.

По-англи́йски means in English, and по-ру́сски in Russian, with the same по on the front.

Now the verb itself. About myself, I say я говорю́: I speak.

It brings a new letter, Ю, which says yu: it's У's twin, the way Я is А's.

To a friend, I'd say ты говори́шь, with the ending ишь.

Ш is the sh from шесть; and the soft sign you met in пять makes no sound here.

About Masha, I'd say она́ говори́т, she speaks; and about me, she'd say он говори́т.

And to a customer, вы говори́те: there's the те again, the вы ending from здра́вствуйте.

So the front, говор, never changes: the ending alone tells you who is speaking.

And as in вода́ from lesson one, an о without the stress sounds like а: говорю́ is ga-va-RYU.

But Tom and I are old friends, so outside the café we say ты.

Ты, so говори́шь; and I answer for myself with я говорю́. Fair enough, Masha: немно́го.

Five questions now, then your score. Match each person to the verb.

Masha tells a friend about Tom: Том … по-ру́сски. Which word fills the gap?

Говори́т: Tom is он, and он takes the ending ит.

A Russian asks: Вы говори́те по-ру́сски? Answer a little: type it.

Немно́го: a little.

You meet a friend's grandmother. How do you ask if she speaks English?

Вы говори́те: you've just met, and she's older, so вы.

Finish the verb for ты: ты говор… Type the ending.

Ишь: ты говори́шь, with the silent soft sign at the end.

You got 5 of 5.

So one verb, four endings: говорю́, говори́шь, говори́т, говори́те, and the ending tells you who is speaking.

And now you can answer the question every Russian will ask you: да, немно́го!
