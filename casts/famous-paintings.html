Who painted it? Match four famous paintings

Match the Mona Lisa, The Starry Night, The Scream and Girl with a Pearl Earring to their painters, and hear how a theft made one world-famous.

Four of the most famous paintings in the world. But who painted which?

Match each painting to its painter: drag from one card to its partner.

Van Gogh painted The Starry Night in 1889, from the view out of his room at an asylum in the south of France.

Munch made The Scream in several versions, in paint and in pastel.

And Vermeer's girl may be no portrait at all, but a study of a face in a fancy costume.

But the most famous of them all was not always so famous.

In August 1911, a former Louvre worker, Vincenzo Peruggia, walked out with the Mona Lisa under his smock.

Newspapers around the world printed her face, and crowds queued to stare at the empty wall. Even Pablo Picasso was questioned by the police.

Two years later Peruggia tried to sell her in Florence, and was caught.

She came home to Paris more famous than ever. The theft made her the world's best-known painting.
