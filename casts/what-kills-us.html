What kills the most people?

Rank five of the world's biggest killers, then see why the heart, the blood vessels and the lungs top the list.

What kills the most people in the world? Here are five of the biggest killers, in no particular order.

Put them in order, the one that kills the most first.

Now count the deaths. Heart disease leads, at about nine million a year.

Stroke follows, at seven million.

Both are diseases of the blood vessels, and together they cause nearly one death in four.

Next come the lungs: COPD, mostly from tobacco and smoky indoor air, and then pneumonia and other lung infections.

Diabetes comes last of these five, but it also feeds the first two, because it damages the blood vessels.
