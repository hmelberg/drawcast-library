Is it a dinosaur?

T. rex, a pterodactyl, a chicken, a mammoth: tap every true dinosaur, and find out why one of them lays your breakfast eggs.

Seven famous animals, and one from the farmyard. Only some of them are dinosaurs.

Which of these animals are dinosaurs? Tap every dinosaur, then Done.

Tyrannosaurus, Triceratops and Stegosaurus are the easy ones.

But the pterodactyl is not a dinosaur. Pterosaurs were flying reptiles, cousins on a branch of their own.

Plesiosaurs swam the seas, another branch of reptiles, and no dinosaurs either.

And Dimetrodon, with the sail on its back, lived before the first dinosaurs, and is closer kin to us mammals.

The mammoth is plainly a mammal: an elephant in a woolly coat.

Which leaves the surprise: the chicken.

Birds descend from the same group of meat-eating dinosaurs as Tyrannosaurus, so biologists count them as dinosaurs.

So the dinosaurs never all died out: about eleven thousand species of them are still flying around us.
