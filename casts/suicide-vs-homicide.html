Around the world, which takes more lives each year: suicide, or homicide?

Tap the one you think takes more lives.

Suicide does, by a wide margin.

Worldwide, about nine people in every hundred thousand die by suicide each year: more than seven hundred thousand lives.

Homicide takes about five and a half per hundred thousand. So suicide claims about two-thirds more lives.

And it is not because suicide is rising. In the early 1990s the rate was about fifteen; it has fallen by some forty percent.

So why does murder feel like the bigger threat? It is news. In three big American news outlets, homicide got forty-three times more coverage than its share of deaths.

Suicide rarely makes the news, so the larger bar is the one we hardly see.

Behind that bar are people. If this touches you or someone close, talk to someone, or find a helpline near you.
