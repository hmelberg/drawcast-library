Here's a puzzle about our neighbour Venus. Like Earth, it spins on its axis, and it circles the Sun.

It does: on Venus the Sun rises in the west and sets in the east.

Why backwards? No one knows for sure. Maybe a giant collision long ago, or the Sun's pull on its thick atmosphere.

And because it spins backwards as it orbits, the two motions add up: from one sunrise to the next takes about a hundred and seventeen Earth days.
