Which languages have the most native speakers?

Rank five great languages by native speakers, then see how the order flips when you count everyone who speaks them.

Five of the world's great languages. Which has the most native speakers, the people who learned it first, as children?

Put these languages in order of native speakers, from most to fewest. Drag the cards, then press Answer.

Mandarin wins by a mile: nearly a billion people grew up speaking it.

Spanish comes second, with almost half a billion: carried across the Atlantic five centuries ago, it is now the home language of most of Latin America.

Then English and Hindi, close together at around three hundred and fifty million or so each.

French, for all its fame, has only about seventy-five million native speakers.

But count everyone who speaks a language, natively or not, and the order changes.

English jumps to first, with about one and a half billion speakers. Most of them learned it as a second language.

So the world's most spoken language is mostly spoken with an accent.
