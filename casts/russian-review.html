A longer Russian story: read it all yourself

Read a nine-sentence Russian story made only of words from lessons 1–9, hear it, test what you understood, and see how far you've come.

Can you read a whole Russian story on your own? This one has nine sentences.

Every word in it comes from lessons one to nine: read it first, and tap any word you're unsure of.

Э́то рестора́н. Стол но́мер семь. Тут моя́ ма́ма, мой па́па и мой брат. Па́па пьёт ко́фе, ма́ма пьёт чай. Брат говори́т: «Суп, пожа́луйста!» Том то́же тут. Он немно́го говори́т по-ру́сски. А где кот? Он то́же тут, на окне́!

It opens with э́то, this is, from lesson eight, and a number from lesson six: стол но́мер семь, table seven, with the soft sign at the end.

Тут, here, from lesson nine: here are my mum, my dad and my brother.

Мой or моя́? The noun decides: моя́ ма́ма, with the red я of a she, then мой брат, ending in a consonant, and мой па́па, still a he.

The drinks are lesson five's: dad drinks coffee, mum drinks tea.

And my brother? Суп, пожа́луйста!

Говори́т is lesson seven's verb: here it means says, and later on, speaks.

Then a surprise: Том то́же тут. That's me: Tom is here too!

Он немно́го говори́т по-ру́сски: he speaks a little Russian.

Only a little? Well, the ending is right: говори́т, with the -ит of он and она.

Last, где, where, from lesson nine: so where's the cat?

Он то́же тут, на окне́: on the window, with the е after на from lesson five.

Now five questions on the story, from memory. What number is the table? Pick one.

Семь, seven: стол но́мер семь.

Who drinks tea? Pick one.

Mum: ма́ма пьёт чай, and dad has the coffee.

What does the brother ask for? Type it in Russian.

Суп: брат говори́т, суп, пожа́луйста!

Who speaks a little Russian? Pick one.

Tom: Том немно́го говори́т по-ру́сски. A little!

Где кот? Where is the cat? Pick one.

On the window: он то́же тут, на окне́.

You got 5 of 5.

Now look back at ten lessons. You can read the letters, and a word's last letter tells you he, she or it.

You can say hello, order in a café, and count to ten.

You can say who speaks what, whose mum it is, and where the cat is.

And you've just read nine sentences on your own: the old words did all the work, and not one word was new.

Спаси́бо и до свида́ния!
