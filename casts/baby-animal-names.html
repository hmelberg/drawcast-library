What are their babies called?

Joey, cygnet, leveret, elver: match four animals to the names of their young, and meet the best baby name of all.

What do you call a baby kangaroo? Or a baby swan? Many animals' young have names of their own.

Match each animal to the name of its young: drag from one card to its partner.

A baby kangaroo is a joey, born about the size of a jelly bean, and raised in its mother's pouch.

A young swan is a cygnet, from cygnus, the Latin for swan.

A young hare is a leveret, from the old French word for a hare.

And a young eel is an elver, swimming in from the sea after a journey of thousands of kilometres.

But the best name of all belongs to the echidna, the spiny egg-laying mammal of Australia. Its baby is a puggle.

Fittingly, the word may have started out as the name of a soft toy.
