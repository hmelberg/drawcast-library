When will the world's population stop growing? Since 1950 it has more than tripled, to eight point two billion. Your guess, and my co-host's.

Easy, I'll go first: it never stops. Twenty-two hundred, at the earliest.

Click on the line where you think the peak comes.

Sorry. The UN's projection says 2084.

Fine. But at the top it'll be huge. Fifteen billion?

And how many people at the peak? Drag the slider.

Ten point three, so two billion more than today. After the peak, a slow decline.

But people have fewer kids now. Why does it keep growing?

They do. In fact the number of children under five already peaked, back in 2017.

Wait. Fewer babies, but more people?

Yes: today's children still have to grow up, and more of us live to old age. The total catches up later.

So the babies peaked first, and the rest of us follow in 2084. I was off by a century.
