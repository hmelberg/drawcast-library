Russian gender from the last letter: он, она, оно

Every Russian noun is a he, a she or an it, and its last letter tells you which: он, она or оно, with the one exception worth knowing.

In Russian, every noun is a he, a she or an it — even a table.

So which is a book, or a window?

In French you would memorise each word; Russian gives you a clue.

Start with three words from lesson one: дом, кот and суп are all он.

And мама and вода, mum and water, are both она.

Before I tell you, make a guess. What decides whether a noun is он or она?

Its last letter: дом, кот and суп end in a consonant, мама and вода end in а.

Here is the clue, at the very end: дом, кот and суп end in a consonant.

And a consonant ending makes a noun он.

Here is a new word: стол, a table.

It ends in Л, a new letter that says l, so a table is он.

Мама and вода end in а, and an а ending makes a noun она.

Книга, a book, brings another new letter: Г, which says g.

It ends in а as well, so a book is она.

Я, which says ya, is а's twin: неделя, a week, is она too.

The third ending is о or е, and it makes a noun оно: it.

Окно is a window, молоко is milk, and море is the sea: all three are оно.

One honest exception: папа, dad, ends in а, yet he is он.

For a man, the meaning wins over the ending.

Now mix them up, the way you will meet them. Put each word in its box: он, она or оно.

Окно́ means window. Type its pronoun.

Оно: окно ends in о.

Type any noun from this lesson that is она.

Книга, мама, вода or неделя: each one ends in а or я.

So you don't learn a gender for every noun: you read it off the last letter, and only a few words, like папа, let the meaning win.

Here are three words you have never seen: парк, a park; лампа, a lamp; and вино, wine.

Which one is оно? Tap it.

Вино ends in о, so wine is оно.

Парк ends in a consonant, so it is он; лампа in а, so она; and вино in о, so оно.

And lesson one's ресторан? It ends in Н, so it is он.

But мама в ресторане means mum is in the restaurant,

and ресторан gained an е: an ending at work.
