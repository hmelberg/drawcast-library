Put every mammal on Earth on one giant scale: cows, people, whales, mice. How much of the weight is wild?

Whales, elephants, all those deer? Half, at least.

Drag the edge of the wild slice to your guess.

Five percent? Every wild mammal on the planet?

Every one. Humans alone are thirty-six percent, seven times all the wild mammals together.

The rest is our farm animals and pets, mostly cattle: fifty-nine percent.

So the wild ones lost a tug of war with cows.

It gets stranger. All the world's dogs weigh about as much as every wild mammal on land.

Let me guess. Pigs against whales?

Right. Farmed pigs weigh about as much as all the whales, seals and dolphins in the sea.

Birds, then. Birds must still be wild.

Not by weight. Chickens and other poultry weigh more than twice all the wild birds put together.
