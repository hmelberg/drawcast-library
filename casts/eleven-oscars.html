Which films won eleven Oscars?

Tap the three films that share the record of eleven Oscars, and find out which one won every award it was up for.

The record for Oscars won by a single film is eleven, and only a handful of films have reached it.

Which of these films won eleven Oscars? Tap every one.

Only three films share the record. Ben-Hur came first, in 1959, chariot race and all.

Titanic matched it in 1997, from fourteen nominations.

West Side Story came painfully close, with ten.

And Avatar, the biggest earner of all, took home just three.

But the third, The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King, did something the others did not.

It won every single award it was nominated for: eleven out of eleven.

And it was the first fantasy film ever to win Best Picture.
