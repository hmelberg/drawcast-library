Which is the tallest mountain on Earth?

Everest is the highest, but is it the tallest? Guess, then watch Mauna Kea rise from the sea floor and meet the summit closest to the stars.

Everest is the highest: its summit stands 8,849 metres above the sea.

Measured from its foot to its summit, which mountain is tallest?

Mauna Kea, in Hawaii: from its foot on the sea floor, it rises about ten thousand two hundred metres.

Above the waves, Mauna Kea is only about 4,200 metres.

But its foot lies some six thousand metres down, on the floor of the Pacific.

Counted from there, it beats Everest by more than a kilometre.

And there is a third champion: Chimborazo, a volcano in Ecuador.

The Earth bulges at the equator, and Chimborazo sits almost on it.

So its summit is the point farthest from Earth's centre,

about two kilometres farther out than Everest's.

But the tallest of them all is mostly underwater.
