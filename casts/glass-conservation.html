Which glass has more water? Start with two identical glasses, filled to exactly the same level.

So do most four-year-olds. They point to the tall glass, because the water is higher.

Even after watching it being poured?

Even then. The Swiss psychologist Jean Piaget made this little test famous.

He called it conservation: knowing that an amount stays the same when its shape changes. Most children get it at around six or seven.
