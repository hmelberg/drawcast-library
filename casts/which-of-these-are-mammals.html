Which of these animals are mammals? Here are eight, from the sea, the air and the land.

Which of these animals are mammals? Tap every mammal.

Whales and dolphins live in the sea and bats fly, yet like the hedgehog they all have hair at some point in life, and feed their young on milk.

The penguin, though, is a bird that swims, the shark a fish, the octopus a mollusc, and the crocodile a reptile.

So to find a mammal, ask about hair and milk, not where it swims or flies.
