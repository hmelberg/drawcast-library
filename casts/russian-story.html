A little story in Russian: мама, папа и кот

Read your first little Russian story, five sentences of words you already know and three new ones, then check what you understood.

Can you already read a Russian story?

Here are five short sentences, nearly all in words you know: read them first, and tap any word you're unsure of.

Ма́ма и па́па в рестора́не.

Ма́ма пьёт ко́фе, па́па пьёт чай.

А кот? Он на столе́.

На столе́ молоко́.

Кот то́же пьёт!

The first sentence is all old words: mum and dad are in the restaurant, and, as in lesson one, Russian needs no word for are.

Then a new word, пьёт, drinks: mum drinks coffee and dad drinks tea, the two drinks from the café.

And the cat? Он на столе́: he's on the table.

Он, he, because кот ends in a consonant: a cat is a he, just as lesson two said.

And стол, the table, has gained an е after на, on: на столе́, just as ресторан became рестора́не after в.

So what's on the table? Молоко́: milk.

Тоже means too, so the last sentence is the punchline: the cat drinks too, and we can guess what.

The colours are lesson two's endings: a red а on мама, a violet о on молоко, and a red а on папа, our one exception, who is still a he.

Now four questions on the story, from memory. Where are mum and dad? Pick one.

In a restaurant: ма́ма и па́па в рестора́не.

True or false: па́па пьёт ко́фе.

False: па́па пьёт чай; it's mum who drinks the coffee.

What is on the table? Type it in Russian.

Молоко́: на столе́ молоко́, there's milk on the table.

Who drinks the milk, do you think? Pick one.

The cat: кот то́же пьёт, and the milk is right there on his table.

You got 4 of 4.

So you've read your first Russian story: old words did most of the work, three new ones filled the gaps, and the endings told you who was a he.

And if the cat could talk, he'd say: молоко́, пожа́луйста! Пока́!
