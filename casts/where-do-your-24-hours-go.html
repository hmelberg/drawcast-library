Where does an average day go?

Split an average day into sleep, work, TV, meals and the rest, then compare your guess with a large American time-use survey.

Where do a day's twenty-four hours go? Everyone gets the same twenty-four, and every one of them has to go somewhere.

How does the average day split? Drag each bar; the bar on the right shows the hours left.

Sleep takes the biggest share, about nine hours, and that is high because the survey counts everyone aged fifteen and over, on every day of the week.

That is also why work and school look small: averaged over weekends, holidays and the retired, they come to under four hours.

Television alone still takes over two and a half hours, and that is before phones and games are counted.

Meals take a little over an hour. Everything else, from chores and shopping to travel and friends, fills the last seven hours or so.
