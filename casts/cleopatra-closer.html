Did Cleopatra live closer in time to the builders of the Great Pyramid, or to the first Moon landing?

Here are the three moments: the pyramid is finished, Cleopatra dies, and Apollo 11 lands.

When did each happen? Drag each card to its year on the timeline.

The Great Pyramid was finished around 2560 BC, for the pharaoh Khufu.

That is about two and a half thousand years before Cleopatra died.

She died in 30 BC.

And from her death to the Moon landing in 1969 is less than two thousand years.

So Cleopatra lived closer to Neil Armstrong than to the pyramid builders.

To her, the pyramids were already ancient: older to her than she is to us.

And she was not Egyptian by descent. Her family, the Ptolemies, were Macedonian Greeks, and Plutarch says she was the first of them to learn Egyptian.
