First: the fax is older than the telephone.

Alexander Bain patented a way to send pictures by telegraph in 1843, thirty-three years before Bell's phone.

His design used two swinging pendulums, kept in step, to scan a picture line by line.

Next, Oxford was teaching students by 1096, more than two centuries before the Aztecs founded their capital, Tenochtitlan.

So when that city rose from its lake, Oxford had already been teaching for generations.

And the Great Pyramid is older than the last woolly mammoths.

The pyramid was finished around 2560 BC.

A small herd survived on Wrangel Island, in the Arctic Ocean, until about four thousand years ago.

So when the pyramid was already five centuries old, mammoths still walked the Earth.
