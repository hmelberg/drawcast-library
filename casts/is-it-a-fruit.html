Is a tomato a fruit? To a botanist, a fruit is the part of a flowering plant that grows from the flower and holds the seeds.

Which of these are fruits, the way a botanist sees it? Tap every fruit.

Tomatoes, cucumbers and chilies all grow from a flower and carry their seeds inside, so to a botanist they are fruits.

Carrots, on the other hand, are roots, potatoes are swollen underground stems, and broccoli is a bunch of flower buds that have not opened yet.

The kitchen still calls the tomato a vegetable, and in 1893 the US Supreme Court agreed, for the sake of import taxes.
