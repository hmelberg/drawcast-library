Which letter is in no US state's name?

J, Q, X or Z: one letter appears in no US state name. Pick it, then meet the lone states that save the other three.

Fifty US states, from Alabama to Wyoming. One of these four letters appears in none of their names.

All four are rare letters in English. But only one is missing completely.

Which letter appears in no US state name? Tap it.

Q. Not one of the fifty states has a Q in its name.

J is rescued by a single state: New Jersey.

Z also hangs on by one: Arizona.

X does a little better, with Texas and New Mexico.

But Q has nobody at all.

The nearest miss is a city: Albuquerque, in New Mexico, which has two Qs of its own.

For a Q, you have to cross the border, to the Canadian province of Quebec.

So Q is the only letter of the alphabet that no state name uses.
