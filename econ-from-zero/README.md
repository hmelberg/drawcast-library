# Economics from zero

A long course that starts from big questions — do markets work, how do countries grow rich, why is there unemployment — and learns the economics needed to answer them, with the debates, exceptions and evidence left in.

1. [Why are some countries rich and others poor?](https://www.drawcast.app/#gh=hmelberg/drawcast-library/econ-from-zero/01-why-are-some-countries-rich-and-others.cast)
   - Why did living standards stay flat for millennia and then shoot up like a hockey stick?
   - Why do the gaps between countries today dwarf anything in history?
2. [What is it that is scarce?](https://www.drawcast.app/#gh=hmelberg/drawcast-library/econ-from-zero/02-what-is-it-that-is-scarce.cast)
   - What does a choice really cost when you could have done something else?
   - What can Harbourtown's frontier of fish and bread tell us about trade-offs?
3. [Is economics a topic or a method?](https://www.drawcast.app/#gh=hmelberg/drawcast-library/econ-from-zero/03-is-economics-a-topic-or-a-method.cast)
   - What do the price of bread, Becker's theory of crime and the tip you leave a waiter have in common?
   - Four quick answers to what economics is — and why this course starts with questions instead
4. [Why use models that are obviously false?](https://www.drawcast.app/#gh=hmelberg/drawcast-library/econ-from-zero/04-why-use-models-that-are-obviously-false.cast)
   - Is a map wrong because it leaves out the trees?
   - What did Friedman mean by judging a model by its predictions, and why do critics disagree?
