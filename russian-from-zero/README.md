# Russian from zero

Learn to read, hear and say your first Russian, one short lesson at a time. Each lesson builds on the ones before: the Cyrillic letters, gender, greetings, the café, numbers, verbs, my family, where things are, and short stories you read yourself. Tap any Russian word to hear it again.

1. [Read Russian in 14 letters](https://www.drawcast.app/#gh=hmelberg/drawcast-library/russian-from-zero/01-read-russian.cast)
   - Which Cyrillic letters look like English ones but sound different?
   - Can you read ресторан, кот and мама by the end?
2. [Russian gender from the last letter: он, она, оно](https://www.drawcast.app/#gh=hmelberg/drawcast-library/russian-from-zero/02-russian-gender.cast)
   - How does the last letter of a noun tell you if it is он, она or оно?
3. [Hello in Russian: привет or здравствуйте?](https://www.drawcast.app/#gh=hmelberg/drawcast-library/russian-from-zero/03-russian-hello.cast)
   - When do you say привет and when здравствуйте?
   - What is the difference between ты and вы?
4. [At the café in Russian: кофе, чай, суп](https://www.drawcast.app/#gh=hmelberg/drawcast-library/russian-from-zero/04-russian-cafe.cast)
   - How do you order кофе or чай, and what does пожалуйста do?
5. [A little story in Russian: мама, папа и кот](https://www.drawcast.app/#gh=hmelberg/drawcast-library/russian-from-zero/05-russian-story.cast)
   - Can you read a short story yourself before you hear it?
   - Why ресторане and столе after в and на?
6. [Count to ten in Russian: один, два, три](https://www.drawcast.app/#gh=hmelberg/drawcast-library/russian-from-zero/06-russian-numbers.cast)
   - How do you count from one to ten and read a phone number?
7. [I speak Russian: я говорю, ты говоришь](https://www.drawcast.app/#gh=hmelberg/drawcast-library/russian-from-zero/07-russian-speak.cast)
   - How does the verb change with я, ты, он and вы?
   - How do you say you speak a little Russian?
8. [My family in Russian: мой, моя, моё](https://www.drawcast.app/#gh=hmelberg/drawcast-library/russian-from-zero/08-russian-family.cast)
   - Why мой папа but моя мама?
9. [Where is it? где, в and на in Russian](https://www.drawcast.app/#gh=hmelberg/drawcast-library/russian-from-zero/09-russian-where.cast)
   - How do you ask where something is and say it is in or on something?
10. [A longer Russian story: read it all yourself](https://www.drawcast.app/#gh=hmelberg/drawcast-library/russian-from-zero/10-russian-review.cast)
   - Can you read a whole story using everything from lessons 1 to 9?
