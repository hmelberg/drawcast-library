# The Painting Knows You're There

Ten paintings from 1434 to 1882 that know someone is looking — mirrors, impossible viewpoints, hidden watchers — and what each one does with you, the viewer.

1. [Who else is in the room? Van Eyck's Arnolfini Portrait](https://www.drawcast.app/#gh=hmelberg/drawcast-library/the-painting-knows-youre-there/01-who-else-is-in-the-room-van-eyck-s.cast)
   - What does the little mirror show that the room itself doesn't?
   - Why would a painter write 'Jan van Eyck was here' on the wall?
2. [Where do you have to stand? Holbein's The Ambassadors](https://www.drawcast.app/#gh=hmelberg/drawcast-library/the-painting-knows-youre-there/02-where-do-you-have-to-stand-holbein-s-the.cast)
   - What is the grey smear across the floor?
   - Why hide death where only a step to the side reveals it?
3. [Where is the hero? Bruegel's Fall of Icarus](https://www.drawcast.app/#gh=hmelberg/drawcast-library/the-painting-knows-youre-there/03-where-is-the-hero-bruegel-s-fall-of.cast)
   - Where is Icarus?
   - Why does nobody in the picture look?
4. [Which one is Matthew? Caravaggio's Calling of Saint Matthew](https://www.drawcast.app/#gh=hmelberg/drawcast-library/the-painting-knows-youre-there/04-which-one-is-matthew-caravaggio-s.cast)
   - Where does the light point, and where does Christ's hand point?
   - Is Matthew the bearded man or the young man counting coins?
5. [Who is the painter painting? Velázquez's Las Meninas](https://www.drawcast.app/#gh=hmelberg/drawcast-library/the-painting-knows-youre-there/05-who-is-the-painter-painting-velazquez-s.cast)
   - Who is everyone in the room looking at?
   - Whose place are we standing in?
6. [Why pull back a curtain? Vermeer's The Art of Painting](https://www.drawcast.app/#gh=hmelberg/drawcast-library/the-painting-knows-youre-there/06-why-pull-back-a-curtain-vermeer-s-the.cast)
   - Who is the curtain pulled back for?
   - Why paint the painter from behind?
7. [Who is watching whom? Fragonard's The Swing](https://www.drawcast.app/#gh=hmelberg/drawcast-library/the-painting-knows-youre-there/07-who-is-watching-whom-fragonard-s-the.cast)
   - Who can see what from where?
   - What makes us part of the joke?
8. [Why show a man from behind? Friedrich's Wanderer above the Sea of Fog](https://www.drawcast.app/#gh=hmelberg/drawcast-library/the-painting-knows-youre-there/08-why-show-a-man-from-behind-friedrich-s.cast)
   - What changes when we see a figure only from the back?
   - Where does the painting put us?
9. [Why does the street walk toward us? Caillebotte's Paris Street; Rainy Day](https://www.drawcast.app/#gh=hmelberg/drawcast-library/the-painting-knows-youre-there/09-why-does-the-street-walk-toward-us.cast)
   - Why are the figures cut off by the frame?
   - Where would we have to be standing?
10. [Can this mirror be right? Manet's A Bar at the Folies-Bergère](https://www.drawcast.app/#gh=hmelberg/drawcast-library/the-painting-knows-youre-there/10-can-this-mirror-be-right-manet-s-a-bar.cast)
   - Why doesn't the reflection match the barmaid?
   - Who is the man in the mirror, and where are we?
