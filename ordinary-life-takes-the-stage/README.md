# Ordinary Life Takes the Stage

Ten paintings from 1560 to 1886 in which ordinary people and everyday work move from the edge of the picture to its centre — and why that was once a provocation.

1. [Why is the ploughman bigger than the hero? Bruegel's Fall of Icarus](https://www.drawcast.app/#gh=hmelberg/drawcast-library/ordinary-life-takes-the-stage/01-why-is-the-ploughman-bigger-than-the.cast)
   - What does Bruegel put in front, and what in the corner?
   - What does that say about whose life matters?
2. [What does a day's work look like? Bruegel's The Harvesters](https://www.drawcast.app/#gh=hmelberg/drawcast-library/ordinary-life-takes-the-stage/02-what-does-a-day-s-work-look-like-bruegel.cast)
   - What is each group doing at this hour?
   - Why paint peasants as the whole subject?
3. [Why set a holy story in a tax office? Caravaggio's Calling of Saint Matthew](https://www.drawcast.app/#gh=hmelberg/drawcast-library/ordinary-life-takes-the-stage/03-why-set-a-holy-story-in-a-tax-office.cast)
   - What makes this room so ordinary?
   - Why did Caravaggio dress the scene in his own time?
4. [How can pouring milk be monumental? Vermeer's The Milkmaid](https://www.drawcast.app/#gh=hmelberg/drawcast-library/ordinary-life-takes-the-stage/04-how-can-pouring-milk-be-monumental.cast)
   - What makes one kitchen task look so important?
   - What do the bread, the jug and the foot warmer tell us?
5. [Why paint a child's game? Chardin's The House of Cards](https://www.drawcast.app/#gh=hmelberg/drawcast-library/ordinary-life-takes-the-stage/05-why-paint-a-child-s-game-chardin-s-the.cast)
   - What is the boy really doing?
   - Why would a painter give a game this much care?
6. [Why was a village funeral a scandal? Courbet's A Burial at Ornans](https://www.drawcast.app/#gh=hmelberg/drawcast-library/ordinary-life-takes-the-stage/06-why-was-a-village-funeral-a-scandal.cast)
   - Who are these people, and how big is the canvas?
   - Why did Paris critics hate it?
7. [What is gleaning? Millet's The Gleaners](https://www.drawcast.app/#gh=hmelberg/drawcast-library/ordinary-life-takes-the-stage/07-what-is-gleaning-millet-s-the-gleaners.cast)
   - What are the three women doing, and what is behind them?
   - Why did some see a threat in it?
8. [Why were these workers 'vulgar'? Caillebotte's The Floor Scrapers](https://www.drawcast.app/#gh=hmelberg/drawcast-library/ordinary-life-takes-the-stage/08-why-were-these-workers-vulgar.cast)
   - What are the men doing, and how do we see them?
   - Why did the Salon turn it away?
9. [Who is the barmaid? Manet's A Bar at the Folies-Bergère](https://www.drawcast.app/#gh=hmelberg/drawcast-library/ordinary-life-takes-the-stage/09-who-is-the-barmaid-manet-s-a-bar-at-the.cast)
   - Who is she, and what is she selling?
   - What does her face say that the crowd behind her doesn't?
10. [Who gets a day off? Seurat's A Sunday on La Grande Jatte](https://www.drawcast.app/#gh=hmelberg/drawcast-library/ordinary-life-takes-the-stage/10-who-gets-a-day-off-seurat-s-a-sunday-on.cast)
   - Who is in the park, and what is each doing?
   - Why paint leisure with such strict order?
