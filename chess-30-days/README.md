# Chess in 30 Days: Break the Plateau

Thirty lessons for players who have stopped improving: the habits that stop the blunders, a bank of tactical patterns, three openings you understand, plans for the middlegame and the endings that decide games. Every lesson has you play the moves on the board.

1. [Why have you stopped getting better?](https://www.drawcast.app/#gh=hmelberg/drawcast-library/chess-30-days/01-why-have-you-stopped-getting-better.cast)
   - Why do the same mistakes keep costing you games?
   - What three questions should you ask before every move?
   - Where do you stand today? (a short scored test)
2. [What did your opponent's last move do?](https://www.drawcast.app/#gh=hmelberg/drawcast-library/chess-30-days/02-what-did-your-opponent-s-last-move-do.cast)
   - Why is reading the opponent's move the first habit of strong players?
   - How do you spot a threat before it lands?
3. [Does this exchange really win?](https://www.drawcast.app/#gh=hmelberg/drawcast-library/chess-30-days/03-does-this-exchange-really-win.cast)
   - How do you count attackers and defenders on a square?
   - Why can a trade that looks equal lose material?
   - What is an in-between move, and why does it break your count?
4. [How far ahead can you see?](https://www.drawcast.app/#gh=hmelberg/drawcast-library/chess-30-days/04-how-far-ahead-can-you-see.cast)
   - Why look at checks, captures and threats first?
   - How do you calculate three moves deep without moving the pieces?
5. [Why do the opening rules exist?](https://www.drawcast.app/#gh=hmelberg/drawcast-library/chess-30-days/05-why-do-the-opening-rules-exist.cast)
   - What do tempo, the centre and king safety buy you?
   - When is it right to break an opening rule?
6. [How do you learn from your own games?](https://www.drawcast.app/#gh=hmelberg/drawcast-library/chess-30-days/06-how-do-you-learn-from-your-own-games.cast)
   - How do you find the move where a game turned?
   - Why keep a list of your own mistakes?
7. [Week 1 review: habits under pressure](https://www.drawcast.app/#gh=hmelberg/drawcast-library/chess-30-days/07-week-1-review-habits-under-pressure.cast)
   - Can you use the three questions when nobody tells you which tactic is coming?
   - Can you finish a won ending against the computer?
8. [How can one move attack two things?](https://www.drawcast.app/#gh=hmelberg/drawcast-library/chess-30-days/08-how-can-one-move-attack-two-things.cast)
   - What makes a fork work: knight, queen and pawn forks?
   - How do you set up a fork that is not there yet?
9. [Why can't that piece move, and what is behind it?](https://www.drawcast.app/#gh=hmelberg/drawcast-library/chess-30-days/09-why-can-t-that-piece-move-and-what-is.cast)
   - What is the difference between an absolute and a relative pin?
   - How do you pile up on a pinned piece and win it?
   - How does a skewer differ from a pin?
10. [Who is guarding what?](https://www.drawcast.app/#gh=hmelberg/drawcast-library/chess-30-days/10-who-is-guarding-what.cast)
   - How do you remove the defender?
   - What does it mean when one piece has too many jobs?
   - How do deflection and decoy pull a piece away?
11. [Which mating patterns should you know by sight?](https://www.drawcast.app/#gh=hmelberg/drawcast-library/chess-30-days/11-which-mating-patterns-should-you-know-by.cast)
   - What do back-rank, smothered, Anastasia's and Arabian mates have in common?
   - Why does the Greek gift sacrifice on h7 work, and when does it fail?
12. [Why do in-between moves wreck your calculation?](https://www.drawcast.app/#gh=hmelberg/drawcast-library/chess-30-days/12-why-do-in-between-moves-wreck-your.cast)
   - What is an in-between move (zwischenzug)?
   - What is a desperado, and why play it?
13. [How do two tactics combine?](https://www.drawcast.app/#gh=hmelberg/drawcast-library/chess-30-days/13-how-do-two-tactics-combine.cast)
   - How do you spot a combination that uses two motifs at once?
   - When does a sacrifice actually work?
14. [Week 2 review: tactics without labels](https://www.drawcast.app/#gh=hmelberg/drawcast-library/chess-30-days/14-week-2-review-tactics-without-labels.cast)
   - Can you find the tactic when the theme is not named?
   - Can you win material from a position against the computer?
15. [What should you play as White?](https://www.drawcast.app/#gh=hmelberg/drawcast-library/chess-30-days/15-what-should-you-play-as-white.cast)
   - What is the idea behind each move of the Italian Game?
   - What is White's plan with c3 and d4?
16. [What should Black play against 1.e4?](https://www.drawcast.app/#gh=hmelberg/drawcast-library/chess-30-days/16-what-should-black-play-against-1-e4.cast)
   - How does 1…e5 meet the Italian with …Bc5?
   - What do you do against the Scotch and other early d4 moves?
17. [What should Black play against 1.d4?](https://www.drawcast.app/#gh=hmelberg/drawcast-library/chess-30-days/17-what-should-black-play-against-1-d4.cast)
   - How does the Queen's Gambit Declined set-up work?
   - What do you do against the London and other quiet systems?
18. [Which opening traps catch club players?](https://www.drawcast.app/#gh=hmelberg/drawcast-library/chess-30-days/18-which-opening-traps-catch-club-players.cast)
   - How do the Fried Liver, Légal's mate and the Blackburne–Shilling trap work?
   - How do you avoid each one, and when can you use them?
19. [What do the pawns tell you?](https://www.drawcast.app/#gh=hmelberg/drawcast-library/chess-30-days/19-what-do-the-pawns-tell-you.cast)
   - What are isolated, doubled, backward and passed pawns good and bad for?
   - How does a pawn chain tell you which side of the board to play on?
20. [Which of your pieces is doing nothing?](https://www.drawcast.app/#gh=hmelberg/drawcast-library/chess-30-days/20-which-of-your-pieces-is-doing-nothing.cast)
   - What makes a piece good or bad: outposts, the bad bishop?
   - Why improve your worst piece first?
   - Why do rooks belong on open files and the seventh rank?
21. [How do you make a plan in the middlegame?](https://www.drawcast.app/#gh=hmelberg/drawcast-library/chess-30-days/21-how-do-you-make-a-plan-in-the-middlegame.cast)
   - How do you find the weakness to aim at?
   - When should you attack the king, and how do pawn storms work when kings castle on opposite sides?
22. [Week 3 review: openings and plans](https://www.drawcast.app/#gh=hmelberg/drawcast-library/chess-30-days/22-week-3-review-openings-and-plans.cast)
   - Can you play your three openings from memory?
   - Can you find the plan in a quiet position?
23. [Can the king catch the pawn?](https://www.drawcast.app/#gh=hmelberg/drawcast-library/chess-30-days/23-can-the-king-catch-the-pawn.cast)
   - What is the rule of the square?
   - What is the opposition, and what are key squares?
24. [What must you know about rook endings?](https://www.drawcast.app/#gh=hmelberg/drawcast-library/chess-30-days/24-what-must-you-know-about-rook-endings.cast)
   - Why does the rook belong behind the passed pawn?
   - How do you hold Philidor's position and win Lucena's?
25. [Bishop or knight in the ending?](https://www.drawcast.app/#gh=hmelberg/drawcast-library/chess-30-days/25-bishop-or-knight-in-the-ending.cast)
   - When is a bishop better than a knight, and when the other way round?
   - How do you trade down into an ending you know is won?
26. [How do you win a won game?](https://www.drawcast.app/#gh=hmelberg/drawcast-library/chess-30-days/26-how-do-you-win-a-won-game.cast)
   - Why trade pieces, not pawns, when you are ahead?
   - How do you take away the opponent's counterplay before you cash in?
27. [How do you save a lost game?](https://www.drawcast.app/#gh=hmelberg/drawcast-library/chess-30-days/27-how-do-you-save-a-lost-game.cast)
   - How do stalemate tricks and perpetual check save half a point?
   - What is a fortress, and how do you make the opponent prove it?
28. [How do you think with the clock running?](https://www.drawcast.app/#gh=hmelberg/drawcast-library/chess-30-days/28-how-do-you-think-with-the-clock-running.cast)
   - How should you split your time across a game?
   - Why do slow games make you better faster?
   - What do you do after a bad loss?
29. [Where does every lesson show up in one game?](https://www.drawcast.app/#gh=hmelberg/drawcast-library/chess-30-days/29-where-does-every-lesson-show-up-in-one.cast)
   - How did Morphy's Opera Game (Paris, 1858) use development, pins and a sacrifice?
   - Which of the course's habits would have saved Black?
30. [Final test: how far have you come?](https://www.drawcast.app/#gh=hmelberg/drawcast-library/chess-30-days/30-final-test-how-far-have-you-come.cast)
   - How do you score now, against the same kind of test as on day 1?
   - What should the next three months of training look like?
